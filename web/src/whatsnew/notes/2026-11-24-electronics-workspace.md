# Electronics: schematic and PCB

There is a new Electronics tab. Draw a schematic with resistors, capacitors, LEDs, pin headers and power symbols, join their pins with wires and net labels, then Push to PCB to make a 2-layer board with a footprint for every part. Switch between Schematic, PCB and 3D from the top of the view; it works on phones and tablets too. Routing, checks and manufacturing files come next.
