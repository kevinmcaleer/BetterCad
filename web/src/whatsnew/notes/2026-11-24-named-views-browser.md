# Named views in the browser

Your saved views now live in a Named views folder at the top of the browser, with Home, Front, Side and Top: click one to look from it, right-click to update, rename or delete it. Fit sits by the view cube's bottom left, and the pick filter, Shaded or Line art, floor grid and colour cycle are in the view cube's menu, which a right-click on empty space opens too.
