# Tall feature panels

Extrude, Revolve, Pattern, Mirror, Fillet, Hole and the other tools now set up in a tall panel at the side of the view, one labelled row per setting, with Cancel and OK at the foot. Profile and Axis show what you've picked, with an × to clear it. On a phone the panel is a sheet along the bottom, and its title folds it away to show more of the model.
