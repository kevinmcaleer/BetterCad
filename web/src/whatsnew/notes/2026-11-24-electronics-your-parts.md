# Make and import electronics parts

Components now has New part: paste a pin list for the symbol, pick a package and type its sizes for the footprint, and save it to your parts. Import KiCad brings in .kicad_sym and .kicad_mod files from makers and other sites, and Edit a copy starts from any library part. Your parts stay in this browser, follow your account when you are signed in, and export as one file.
