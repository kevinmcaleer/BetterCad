# Check and export a schematic

The schematic now has a rules check that finds unconnected pins, clashing outputs and missing power, says what to do about each, and runs before Push to PCB. You can copy, paste and duplicate with fresh names, replace a part and keep its wires, renumber, type values as 4k7 or 100n, and edit every part in one table. Export saves the schematic as a PDF, an SVG or a KiCad netlist.
